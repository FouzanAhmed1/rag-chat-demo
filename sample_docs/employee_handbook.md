# Meridian Outdoors - Employee Handbook (excerpts)

Welcome to Meridian Outdoors! This handbook covers the basics of working here.
It applies to all full-time and part-time employees.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees earn 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO accrues
monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month, starting on your first day of work.
You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year; anything
above that is forfeited on January 1.

To request PTO, submit your request in the HR portal at least two weeks in
advance for trips of three days or more. Your manager must approve all PTO
requests. Part-time employees earn PTO pro-rated by their scheduled hours.

## Remote work

Meridian Outdoors is hybrid-first. Most roles work from the office two days per
week (Tuesday and Thursday are the standard anchor days) and remotely the rest
of the week. Fully remote arrangements are possible with director approval and
are reviewed once per year.

## Office hours

Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern, Monday through Friday. Outside of
core hours you may set your own schedule with your manager's agreement. The
Burlington, Vermont headquarters is open 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays.
