# Meridian Outdoors - IT FAQ

Common questions for the IT helpdesk. For anything not covered here, email
helpdesk@meridianoutdoors.example or call extension 4357.

## How do I reset my password?

You can reset your own password at any time through the IT self-service portal
at https://it.meridianoutdoors.example/reset. Click "Forgot password", enter
your work email, and follow the link sent to your inbox. The reset link expires
after 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder, then
contact the helpdesk.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of letters,
numbers, and symbols. You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## How do I set up the VPN?

Download the Meridian VPN client from the software center on your laptop, then
sign in with your work email and password plus your 2FA code. The VPN is
required to reach internal tools when you are working remotely. If the client
shows "certificate expired", reinstall it from the software center to get a
fresh certificate.

## How do I request a new laptop?

Laptop requests go through the IT ticket system. New hires automatically get a
laptop provisioned before their start date. If your current laptop is more than
3 years old or has a hardware problem, open a ticket with the category
"Hardware refresh" and IT will schedule a swap within 5 business days.
