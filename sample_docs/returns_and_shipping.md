# Meridian Outdoors - Returns and Shipping Policy

Last updated: March 2026.

## Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be
unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number or
receipt. To start a return, visit https://www.meridianoutdoors.example/returns
and print the prepaid return label. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

Final-sale items marked "clearance" cannot be returned. Gift cards are not
refundable but never expire.

## Shipping

Orders over $75 ship free within the continental United States. Orders under
$75 have a flat $6.95 shipping fee. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business
days; expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout for $14.95.

We currently ship to the US and Canada. Canadian orders ship via standard
international post and take 7 to 14 business days.
