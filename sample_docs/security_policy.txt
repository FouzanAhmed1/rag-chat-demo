Meridian Outdoors - Information Security Policy (summary)

1. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required on every work account,
   including email, VPN, code hosting, and the HR portal. Use an
   authenticator app; SMS codes are allowed only as a backup.

2. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never
   ask for your password by email, chat, or phone.

3. Report phishing emails with the "Report phishing" button in your mail
   client, or forward them to security@meridianoutdoors.example. Do not
   click links or open attachments in suspicious messages.

4. Lock your laptop (Win+L or Ctrl+Cmd+Q) whenever you step away. Full-disk
   encryption must stay enabled on all company devices.

5. If you suspect a security incident, such as a lost laptop or a breached
   account, notify security@meridianoutdoors.example immediately, then open
   a P1 ticket in the IT system.
